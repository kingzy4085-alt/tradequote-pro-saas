# TradeQuote PRO — cold email sequence

Four short emails per contractor, sent from your business address (not a personal Gmail) once the app is live.
Each one is about one pain point and asks for a free trial, never a call. Stop the sequence the moment someone replies or unsubscribes.

**Send rules (Canada's anti-spam law, CASL)**
- Only send to addresses the company publishes on its own website or in a directory, and only about their business. Skip any site that says "no solicitation".
- Every email must show your business name, a mailing address (a PO box is fine), and a working unsubscribe link. Honour unsubscribes within 10 business days. Keep them on a do-not-contact list forever.
- No more than about 30 new contacts a day per sending address when you start, so your domain doesn't land in spam.
- Keep a record of where each address came from (the `source_url` column in the prospect list does this).

## Replies

When someone replies, I draft an answer for you to approve. It always offers to answer in writing or by a recorded screen walkthrough, never a call. Common replies and the angle to take:

| They say | Answer with |
|---|---|
| "We use Procore / another tool" | Ask what they wish it did better for electrical work. Point to the flat CAD price and the field logs. |
| "Too busy right now" | Offer to set up their company and import their customer list for them. |
| "How much?" | A link to /pricing plus the founding offer. |
| "Can I see it?" | A link to the 2–3 minute demo video plus the trial link. |
| "Remove me" | Unsubscribe them right away and send no reply. |
