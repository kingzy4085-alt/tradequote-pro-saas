# TradeQuote PRO

## Bid it, run it, bill it, and know what you made.

**One app for electrical and industrial contractors in Western Canada.** Built for shops of 2 to 60 people doing industrial, shutdown, plant maintenance, commercial and instrumentation work in SK, AB, MB and BC.

### One job, start to finish, without re-typing

**Estimate → Proposal → Job → Timecards & change orders → Invoice → Job cost**

- **Estimate.** Material and labour hours per line. Your labour rate, markup and tax are applied the same way on every bid. Prints with a signature block.
- **Proposal** (launching). One click from the estimate. Your client signs from a secure link and never sees your cost or markup.
- **Job** (launching). The accepted proposal becomes the job. Contract value and budget carry over.
- **Timecards** (launching). The foreman enters crew hours from a phone and approves them, and approved cards lock. Overtime is costed at your multiplier.
- **Change orders** (launching). Priced from your rates, numbered and signed on the spot. The revised contract updates.
- **Invoice** (launching). Percent complete, approved change orders, holdback at the rate you set, and tax, with an invoice number on it.
- **Job cost** (launching). Approved hours and supplier bills roll up against the budget. The job flags At Risk at 85% and Over Budget at 100%.

### Built for the field and the safety binder

- **Daily logs** from the truck: crew, hours, weather, work done, delays and photos. The client rep signs on screen, the log locks, and it prints to PDF.
- **LOTO permits** (launching) sign off in order (locked out, verified zero energy, released), lock on the final signature, and print.
- Tailgate meetings, safety logs, inspections, punch list, tool crib and more, all by job.
- Big buttons that work with gloves on. Installs on Windows, Android and iPhone. It needs a data connection.

### Nothing slips

- **Command Center** shows sub insurance, employee tickets and warranties that expire in the next 30 days.
- **Morning alerts** (launching): WCB clearances expiring, invoices overdue, LOTO permits left open, timecards waiting. On your screen and in your inbox.
- **AI assistant** for Canadian code and construction questions. It cites the edition and section so you can check it.
