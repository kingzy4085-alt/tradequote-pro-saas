# Listing copy, platform by platform

Paste-ready. Limits were checked on 2026-11-18 against the sources named in each section. Where I could not confirm a number it says so: type the copy into the live field and watch the counter. Every price below is **confirm before publishing** (placeholder in `lib/plan.ts`).

Rules for all listings:

- Describe only what the app does today. The NEW features (regional cost engine, calculators, AI estimator, AI assistant, /tools, /demo) go in a listing only after you have checked each one in production.
- No ratings, review counts, customer counts, awards or "trusted by" lines. Leave the field empty until there is real proof.
- Use real screenshots of the real interface (Capterra and G2 require this). Use demo data only: no real client names, no real phone numbers.
- `{site_url}` = your live domain. `{support_email}` = a mailbox someone reads.

## 10. Apple App Store (native wrapper)

- **Risk:** Apple's guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality) can reject a thin web wrapper. I searched for the current wording and did not find a source I could cite, so **verify guideline 4.2 before paying for a wrapper**. Reduce risk by adding something native (push notifications for alerts, camera for photos, share sheet), or skip the store and keep "Add to Home Screen" instructions.
- **Account/fees:** Apple Developer Program is US$99 per membership year (Apple's enrollment page, in local currency where available). Organizations need a legal entity (no DBAs or trade names), a D-U-N-S number, a work email on your domain and a working public website. Individuals use their legal name as seller. A wrapper needs a Mac with Xcode, or a hosted build service.
- **Limits (Apple, via 2026 references):** name 2 to 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100 (Apple's Help says "bytes" in one place, "characters" in another; stay under 100 bytes), description 4,000, promotional text 170 (editable any time, not used for ranking), What's New 4,000.
- **Name (14):** TradeQuote PRO
- **Subtitle (29):** Estimates for Canadian trades
- **Keywords (99):** estimating,electrical,contractor,quote,invoice,holdback,timecard,LOTO,job costing,calculator,Canada
  Do not repeat words that are already in the name or subtitle, and no spaces after commas.
- **Promotional text (under 170):** Free trade calculators and a 14-day trial. Price, sign and run Canadian trade jobs from your phone.
- **Description:** the long description above.
- **Category:** Business (primary), Productivity (secondary).
- **Support URL, marketing URL, privacy URL:** `{site_url}/support` (create it), `{site_url}`, `{site_url}/legal/privacy`.
- **In-app purchase:** none. Subscription is bought on your website. Apple's rules on steering users to outside payment differ by country and change often: **verify current rules for Canada before the first review** and describe the app as "sign in to your existing account" with a free trial created on the web.
- **Review notes:** give a demo login with a pre-filled sample company.
- **Screenshots:** 6.9-inch iPhone set (check current required sizes in App Store Connect), shots 4, 6, 8, a calculator, the pipeline.
