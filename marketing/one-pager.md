## Price it. Get it signed. Know what it cost.

**Estimating and job control for Canadian electrical, industrial and general trade contractors.**

### Price the job from real numbers

- Estimates from saved assemblies, with markup and tax.
- **Regional cost engine:** reference material prices and provincial labour rates with burden. Your own price book overrides them.
- **About 20 trade calculators** that drop priced lines into the estimate: voltage drop, conductor sizing, conduit and box fill, motor circuits, CEC 8-200 dwelling load, EV charger circuits, transformer sizing, lighting layout, concrete, drywall, paint, framing, roofing, flooring, insulation, heat loss, water heater sizing.
- **AI estimator:** turns a scope or drawing into draft priced lines. You review every line.
- Non-productive time allowance for lockout, orientation and permits, plus a provincial permit line.

### Win it

- Proposals your client accepts and e-signs from a link, no account. Line items show at sell price on the client portal.
- Quote pipeline with follow-ups and win rate.

### Run it and bill it

- Change orders with client signature.
- Progress invoices with holdback and your GST/HST number; partial payments; bookkeeper CSV export.
- Job costing from approved timecards. Timecards with approval and payroll export.

### Keep the field safe

- Daily logs with photos. LOTO permits, tailgate meetings and safety inspections with sign-off.
- Alerts for overdue invoices, expiring insurance and WCB clearances.
- File attachments on drawings and documents. Your logo on your documents.
