## Delivery and sound

- **Voice:** one real voice, preferably yours or a Canadian tradesperson's, warm and plain, not an announcer. If you use AI voice, say so in the video description. Record two takes; pick the less polished one.
- **Music:** 90 to 100 bpm, light percussion, a clean electric-guitar or soft-synth loop. Nothing cheesy, no stock "corporate" swell. Duck music 10 dB under voice. Use a licence you can prove: a royalty-free library with commercial rights, kept as a receipt.
- **Sound effects:** one soft click on each screen change; a short chime at the signature. Subtle.
- **Captions:** burned in, high contrast, bottom third, large enough for a phone (at least 5% of frame height). Always add captions because most feeds autoplay muted.
- **End card:** logo, one URL, no phone number.

## Three hook variations (A/B test the first 3 seconds)

| Hook | Voice-over | Caption | Why test it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Problem | Still retyping your estimate into a proposal? | Retyping estimates? Stop. | Speaks to the existing pain |

**How to test:** post the same 15-second cut with each hook on one platform in the same time slot across three days (or use the platform's split test if it has one), same caption and hashtags. Compare 3-second hold, completion rate and clicks to the link. Run each for at least 3 days. Keep the winner. Do not draw conclusions from fewer than a few hundred views per variant.
