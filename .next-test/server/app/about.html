About TradeQuote PRO

Industrial Shutdown & Electrical Contracting Operations Hub

TradeQuote PRO is one app for running an electrical or industrial contracting business — from the first client call to the final bill.

It is built for Canadian contractors: estimators who are tired of re-typing spreadsheets, office staff chasing paperwork, and field crews who need big buttons that work with gloves on and in sunlight.

What's inside

Bidding

CRM, estimates, proposals, RFQs and RFPs

Project controls

Change orders, RFIs, submittals, drawings, documents

Field

Daily, field, safety and tailgate logs, LOTO permits, punch list, timecards

Money

Job costing, progress billing, invoices, payables, receivables

Resources

Crew schedule, tool crib, fleet, equipment rentals, warehouse inventory, vendors

Close-out

Commissioning, cable testing, warranty, defect liability, sub compliance
