How to Use TradeQuote PRO

Six steps from first call to final bill.

  1. 1

    Add your client

    Type the company, contact, phone and job site. Press Save.

    Open CRM →
  2. 2

    Build an estimate

    Press "+ New Estimate". Enter the project, client and your markup %. Press Save Estimate.

    Open Estimates →
  3. 3

    Send a proposal

    Enter the title, amount and scope of work. Press Issue. Use Print / Save PDF to send it.

    Open Proposals →
  4. 4

    Run the job

    Each day your crew adds a daily log, safety log and timecards. LOTO permits and tailgate meetings live here too.

    Open Daily Logs →
  5. 5

    Track changes

    Log every change order and RFI the moment it happens so nothing is lost at billing time.

    Open Change Orders →
  6. 6

    Bill and close out

    Add progress bills as work is done. Finish with warranty and commissioning records.

    Open Progress Billing →

Good to know

  • •Every module works the same way: type a name, pick a status, press Add Record. Your list is below the form.
  • •To remove a record, press Delete on its row.
  • •The Dashboard counts everything at a glance. Click any module tile to jump straight to it.
  • •If a save fails you will see "Save failed" with the reason — nothing fails silently.
  • •On a phone or tablet, install the app (see Settings) so it opens full-screen like any other app.
